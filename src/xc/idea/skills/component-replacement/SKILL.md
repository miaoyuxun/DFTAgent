---
name: component-replacement
description: Replace a weak component with a better-matched, theory-grounded mechanism while keeping the same functional slot interpretable.
---

## applicability
- harmful
- redundant
- exchange
- correlation
- unresolved
- open
- inconclusive

## atomic_blueprint
- replace
- rewire
- enforce

## guardrails
- Replace one slot at a time.
- Preserve a clear causal story for the replacement.
- Name the unresolved scientific question when evidence is ambiguous.
- Transfer one principle, not a bundle of unrelated mechanisms.
- Keep the replacement physically interpretable.
- State the smallest validation that can falsify the replacement.
- Re-run ablation on the replaced slot.

## required_protocols
- ablation
- regression
- stress

## execution_logic
1. Select the weakest supported slot from current findings.
2. If the evidence leaves an unresolved explanatory gap, name it explicitly.
3. Choose one local or theory-backed replacement with a clearer physical role.
4. Replace the closest weak slot and state the falsification test.
5. Rewire only what the replacement requires.
